# Offer 4 — Fill the Bucket ($297/mo + ad spend)

**Pillar:** 7. Marketing Director (paid ads + instant follow-up)
**Promise (client-facing):** Once your follow-up is airtight, we run paid ads to bring in more leads, and every lead gets an instant reply.

## Gate (hard rule)
Sell this only to a location where **Offer 1 (Front Door) and Offer 2 (Front Desk) are live and passed their go-live tests.** Offer 3 is recommended first but not required.
If a client asks for ads first, say: "We fix the leaks first so your ad money isn't wasted." Offer 1 or 2 instead.

## Why this one stands alone
It's the only offer that spends the client's money, and it depends on the others. Keeping it separate keeps the $297 management fee clean and makes the gate easy to enforce.

## What's included
- Campaign setup and management on one platform to start (Meta)
- Ad leads feed straight into `FD-01 New Lead` (instant text-back, booking, reminders)
- Monthly creative refresh; weekly numbers in the client report: spend, leads, cost per lead, booked `{BookingName}`

## Money rules
- Ad spend is billed by the ad platform **to the client's own card**. It never passes through us and is never bundled into the $297
- Ads account owned by the client, agency gets partner access (they keep their ad history if they leave)
- No guaranteed lead counts, cost per lead or revenue. Pilot language until real numbers exist

## Build
1. Confirm gate (Offers 1 + 2 passed) and record the dates
2. Client grants partner access to their ad account; pixel/conversion events connected to the booking
3. Lead form → `FD-01`; tag `lead-ads`
4. Campaign created **paused**; Todd approves budget in writing before anything spends

## Go-live test
Submit a test ad lead (platform test tool) → lands in sub-account → instant text to Todd's number → book → shows in report.

## Legal gates
- [ ] Ad lead forms include the same SMS consent language as Offer 1
- [ ] Ad claims honest: no invented stats, no "guaranteed", no fake urgency
- [ ] Franchisees: franchisor approval for ads confirmed in writing
