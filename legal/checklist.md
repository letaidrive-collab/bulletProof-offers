## Business-level
- [ ] Client agreement covers: pilot terms, $297/offer billing, cancellation, exit ownership (agency owns the system; client owns their business data and gets an export on exit), referral credits ("give a month, get a month"), data clause
- [ ] Signed agreement + card on file before any build (friends and family too)
- [ ] Privacy policy + SMS terms pages live on every client site
- [ ] Hawaii GET handling confirmed with CPA
- [ ] "FastFwd Future" trademark filing once revenue starts
- [ ] Quiet-hours and state-specific texting rules confirmed by the attorney

## Questions for the attorney

3. Recording disclosure wording for callers in all-party-consent states.
4. Referral reward wording ("give a month, get a month") and the disclosure language.
